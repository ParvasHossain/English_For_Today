### ❌ High-Priority Error Log

Keep a running record of your most critical recurring mistakes here to avoid making them on test day. 

* **Grammar:** Watch out for subject-verb agreement in complex sentences (e.g., "The collection of articles *were* modified" ❌ -> *was* modified ✅).
* **Vocabulary:** Avoid using overused words like "good", "bad", "important". Replace them with "beneficial", "detrimental", "paramount".
* **Reading:** Do not assume or use outside knowledge for *True/False/Not Given* questions. Rely strictly on text evidence.
